The idea

Say the size. Get that size.

Frames, wallets, lockets and walls all come in a size. Just So starts from that measurement: say how big the photo should be and the page is drawn to match, for one photo, a sheet full of them, or a poster across several sheets.

Print to size

One photo, as big as you say

Type the size in inches, centimetres or millimetres, or pick a ready-made one such as 4 × 6, 5 × 7, wallet or passport. Crop by dragging and pinching, turn the photo, or cut it as an oval for lockets and badges.

Many on one sheet

Each photo at its own size

Put several photos on one sheet, each at its own size, or make copies that fill the sheet to save paper. Optional corner trim marks or outlines show where to cut.

Posters

One photo across ordinary sheets

Print one photo large across ordinary sheets of paper, with overlap for joining, trim marks, sheet names such as A1 and A2, and a guide page that shows how to put it together.

Screens

See the sheet before it prints.

Each photo shows its size on the page, with a plain-words sharpness rating, Crisp, Good or Soft, and its pixels per inch. Light and dark themes.

Printer check

Most printers shrink the page a little. Just So corrects for it.

Most printers quietly shrink a page by a few per cent, which is why a print can come out slightly small. The printer check measures the error once, and from then on Just So cancels it.

Questions

Good to know

Why did my print come out slightly small?

Most printers quietly shrink a page by a few per cent. Run the printer check: Just So prints a page with two measured lines, you measure them with a ruler and type the readings, and from then on everything for that paper is drawn to cancel the error. The support page has more.

Which printers does it work with?

Any printer Android can use, through Android's own print dialog. You can also save or share a PDF with true page sizes and print it from a computer at 100% or "actual size".

Can I print at a photo lab or kiosk?

Yes. With the full version, Just So saves each sheet as a 300 ppi JPEG picture that a photo lab or kiosk can print.

Does it need an account or access to my photos?

No. There is no account, and photos are chosen with Android's own photo picker, so the app needs no photo or storage permission. Your photos never leave the phone unless you print, save or share them. See the privacy policy.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 9 and later.

Is the full version a subscription?

No. It is a single one-time purchase through Google Play. To restore it, open Settings, tap "I already paid" and use the same Google account.
