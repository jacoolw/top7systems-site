Common questions

My print came out small

Most printers quietly shrink a page to fit inside their margins, often by several per cent. On the inkjet we tested, an unchecked 10 by 15 cm photo came out about 9.4 by 14.2 cm. The printer check is there to fix it. Open Check your printer from the home screen, choose your paper and print the check page. It has two measured lines. Measure them with a ruler, type the readings into Just So and save. From then on, everything for that paper is drawn to cancel the error. The check is kept for each paper size, so do it once for each paper you print on. It belongs to the printer you measured, so run it again if you change printers. If the second check page is still a little out, type the new readings in as well, and Just So refines the correction.

My printer's own app has "Fit to page" or a scaling setting

Some printers' own apps and print services add a setting such as Fit to page, Scale to fit or a scaling percentage. Where you can, turn it off or set it to 100%, so the printer changes the page as little as possible. Then run the printer check with the settings you will use for your photos. The check measures whatever the printer still does to a page, so keep those settings the same afterwards, and run the check again if you change them.

My black ink is empty or not working

Turn on Print in colour under the Print the check page button, or Marks in colour in Settings. The check page, trim marks and labels are then drawn in red, which needs colour ink only. Some printers refuse to print at all while a failed cartridge is installed. On many two-cartridge printers, taking the failed one out lets the printer carry on with the other.

Printing a saved PDF from a computer

Save the PDF from inside Just So: tap the share button beside Print, then Save as PDF. The Save as PDF choice inside Android's own print window is drawn with your printer's correction once you have run the check, so it is not true size.

A PDF saved from Just So has true page sizes. When you print it from a computer, choose 100% or Actual size in the print window, not Fit, Fit to page or Shrink to fit, and choose the same paper size the sheet was made for.

Why is there a margin at the edge of the paper?

Most printers cannot print right to the edge of the paper. The margin keeps your photos clear of that strip, so nothing is cut off. To choose a narrower or wider margin, tap the paper name at the top of the sheet and look for Margin at the paper's edge.

I already paid, but the full version is locked

Open Settings and tap I already paid while the phone is signed in to Google Play with the same Google account that made the purchase. If Just So says it could not reach Google Play, check the connection and try again in a moment.

How do I join a poster?

Lay the sheets out in rows using the names printed on them: A1, A2 and so on from left to right, then B1, B2 on the next row. On every sheet except A1, trim the white left or top edge where the note at the top of the sheet says. Lay each sheet over its neighbour until the pictures line up, then tape or glue. The guide page, printed as the last sheet, shows how the pieces go together.

What do Crisp, Good and Soft mean?

They say how sharp the photo will look at the size you chose, with its pixels per inch alongside. Crisp has plenty of detail for the size. Good is fine at arm's length. Soft means the photo is small for that size and may look blurry, so print it smaller or use a larger photo.
