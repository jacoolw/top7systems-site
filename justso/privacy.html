Just So: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") built Just So, an app for Android that prints photos at exactly the size you ask for. It was designed to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains how Just So handles information. The short version: Just So has no account, collects no personal data, shows no adverts and has no analytics. Your photos stay on your phone unless you print, save or share them.

No Data Collected or Shared by Us

Just So does not send your photos, or any data about you, to us. The app's own code makes no network requests, and it includes no analytics, advertising or tracking software. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

Your Photos

  • Choosing them: photos are chosen with Android's own photo picker, so Just So needs no photo or storage permission.
  • Where they are kept: each photo you choose is copied into the app's private storage on your phone, so that it can be sized, laid out and printed.
  • Where they go: they never leave the device unless you print, save or share them. When you do, the pages go where you send them: to the printer you pick in Android's print dialog, to the file you save, or to the app you share with. What happens to them after that is up to the printer, service or app you chose.

Other Data That Stays on the Device

Alongside your photos, Just So keeps a few things of its own in the app's private storage on your phone: the sheet or poster you are working on, your choices such as units, paper size and theme, and your printer check readings. None of it is sent to us.

Internet Use

The app's own code makes no network requests. The only network use comes from Google Play Billing, described below, which Google uses to handle the optional purchase.

Google Services

  • Google Play Billing: Google uses it to handle the optional one-time purchase and to restore it later. The purchase is made with Google, through Google Play, and not with us.

This is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Just So needs no photo or storage permission, because photos are chosen with Android's own photo picker and printing goes through Android's own print dialog. It asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. The only permissions in the app are the ones that come with Google Play Billing, for billing and network access, which Google uses to handle the optional purchase.

Backups and New Devices

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Just So's settings (units, paper size, theme and printer check readings) are included in your device backup, like any app's settings, and move to a new phone when you restore from it. Your photos are not included. Those backups are made and managed by Google under your Google account's settings; they are not sent to us.

Purchases

Just So is free for one photo per sheet, at any size and with unlimited prints, for posters up to four sheets, for the printer check and for saving PDFs. A single one-time purchase unlocks many photos and copies per sheet, posters of any size, and saving sheets as pictures. The purchase is handled by Google through Google Play. There is no subscription.

Children

Just So collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Data Deletion

Because everything is stored only on your phone, you are in control of it. Remove photos in the app, or remove everything at once by uninstalling it: uninstalling removes everything the app stored. PDFs and pictures that you saved or shared outside the app stay where you put them until you delete them. The purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored from Settings with "I already paid".

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@top7systems.net. Top 7 Systems is based in Australia.
