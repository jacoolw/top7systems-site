Encore: the Guide

What Encore is for

Encore is a reminder that rings again until you mark it done. You set a time; at that time it rings (the first call); if you do not deal with it, it rings again after a few minutes (the encore), and keeps calling until you tap Done, snooze it, or it reaches the number of calls you allowed. There is no account and no server. Everything lives on your phone. This page explains each part of the app. If something is not covered, email support@top7systems.net.

Setting a reminder

Tap New reminder on the home screen. The sheet has one field and a grid of times.

1

Type it the way you would say it

"Call the plumber at 2:30", "Check the oven in 20m", "Pay the car rego tomorrow 9am", "Bins fri 7am", "Water the garden tonight". Encore picks the time out of the sentence and shows what it understood underneath the field: "Understood as today, 2:30 pm. Title: Call the plumber." If it got it wrong, tap a time from the grid instead and the typed words stay as the title. It understands at 3pm, 2:30, in 20m, in 1h 30m, today, tomorrow, tonight, noon, midnight, morning, afternoon, evening, next week and the days of the week. A time that has already passed today is taken to mean tomorrow.

2

Or tap a time

The grid has nine quick times: In 10 min, In 30 min, In 1 hour, Tonight 7pm, Tomorrow 9am, Tomorrow 6pm, Monday 9am, Saturday 10am and Next week. Date and time opens a calendar and a clock for anything else.

3

Choose the encore

The Encore row sets how many minutes pass between calls: 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60. Below it, the counter sets how many calls Encore makes before it goes quiet, from one to ten, or Until done, which keeps calling for as long as it takes. The defaults are five minutes and five calls; change them for every new reminder in Settings.

4

Repeat, alert and note

Repeat makes it come back daily, on weekdays, weekly or monthly. Alert chooses sound, vibrate only, or silent. Note adds a line that shows when it rings. Then tap Set for 2:30 pm.

When it rings

Each call is a notification with three buttons. Done ends the calls and moves the reminder to your history. Snooze 10 starts again in ten minutes, as a fresh first call. Later opens the app with the quick-time grid, so you can pick any time. The first call uses the normal notification sound; the second call onwards uses the Encore calls channel, which rings like an alarm. You can change either channel's sound in Android's notification settings for Encore.

With Full-screen alerts turned on in Settings, the second call onwards fills the lock screen with the encore: the time inside its rings, how long until the next call, and the same three choices. Stop asking about this one at the bottom keeps the reminder but stops it ringing.

The home screen

The headline says what today looks like: "Two due today. One is asking again." A reminder that is calling sits at the top as a midnight card with its rings: a dot for the first call, one more ring for each call since, and an outer arc that drains towards the next call. Done, Snooze 10 and Later are on the card. Below it, what is due today, tomorrow and later, with its time and encore settings. Tap any row to change it. After Done, a sage strip shows what you finished and how many calls it took, with Undo for a few seconds.

A reminder that reached its last call without a Done goes quiet and stays on the home screen as a small card, so nothing is lost. Tap Done when it is, or Later to set a new time.

The Reliability screen

Android has a few switches that can stop any reminder from ringing on time. Open Settings → Reliability to see them in one place, each with a Fix button that opens the right page in Android's settings.

  • Notifications: Encore cannot show a call without them.
  • Alarms and reminders: the permission that lets Encore ring at the exact minute. Without it a call can be up to ten minutes late. Encore asks for it during setup; it is one switch on the Android page the button opens.
  • Battery: set Encore to Unrestricted so the phone never puts it to sleep. On Samsung phones, also add Encore to Never sleeping apps under Settings → Battery → Background usage limits; Samsung puts an app unused for a few days to sleep, and a sleeping app cannot ring.
  • Full-screen alerts: optional. Shows the encore over the lock screen.

Below the checks are the last three calls and how late each one was, to the second, and Test: ring in one minute, which sets a one-minute reminder so you can lock the phone and watch it work.

History

The clock icon on the home screen opens everything you have marked done, by day, with how many calls each one took. The home screen's tiles count this week's finishes, how many needed an encore, and how many went quiet unanswered.

Themes

Settings → Look offers three themes. House lights is Encore's own: parchment, navy ink and one amber accent. Wallpaper takes its colours from your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer while keeping Encore's warm surfaces. Calm turns the amber down to a quieter brass. Dark mode, called Backstage, can follow the system or be forced on or off; it is never pure black. In the evening the parchment warms by a shade, the house lights dimming.

Encore Full

Everything is open for the first fourteen days. After that the free version keeps three reminders at a time; Encore Full is a single one-time purchase made inside the app through Google Play that removes the limit for good. It is not a subscription.

1

Open Settings

Tap the gear on the home screen. The card at the top shows how many trial days are left and the price in your currency once Google Play has answered.

2

Tap Unlock Encore

Google Play's own purchase sheet appears. Confirm with the payment method on your Google account. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card details.

3

Restore on another phone

On a new or reset device, open the same card and tap Restore a purchase while signed in to Google Play with the account that bought it. The unlock comes back at no charge.

Data and privacy

Encore has no account and no server. Reminders, settings and history stay in the app's private storage on your phone. The only network use is Google Play Billing, for the purchase. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The full details are in the privacy policy.
