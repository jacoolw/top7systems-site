Encore: Support

Get in touch

Email support@top7systems.net. Tell us your phone model and Android version, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. A screenshot of Settings → Reliability answers most questions in one go. The guide explains every part of the app.

Common questions

It rang late, or not at the minute I set

Android only lets an app ring at an exact minute when the Alarms and reminders permission is on; without it a call can be up to ten minutes late. Open Settings → Reliability in Encore and tap Fix on that row, which opens the Android page with the switch. The Reliability screen also shows the last three calls and how late each one was.

It showed a notification but made no sound

Three things to check. First, the phone's sound mode: on silent or vibrate, calls vibrate but do not ring. Second, the notification channels: Encore uses Reminders for the first call and Encore calls for the second call onwards; Android lets you silence either one, and the Android notification settings for Encore show both. Third, the reminder's own alert setting on its sheet: Sound, Vibrate or Silent.

It stopped after five calls

That is the default: five calls, then Encore goes quiet and leaves the reminder on the home screen. Open the reminder and change the counter under Encore to more calls or to Until done, or change the default for new reminders in Settings under Calls before it goes quiet.

On my Samsung it stopped ringing after a few days

Samsung phones put apps that have not been opened for a few days to sleep, and a sleeping app cannot ring. In Android Settings, open Apps → Encore → Battery and choose Unrestricted; then open Settings → Battery → Background usage limits and add Encore to Never sleeping apps. The Reliability screen's Battery row shows the first of these and opens the right page.

The encore does not fill the lock screen

Full-screen alerts are off unless you turn them on in Encore's Settings, and Android asks you to allow them on its own page the first time. The first call never goes full screen by design; the second call onwards does. If the phone was unlocked and in use when the call came, Android shows a banner at the top instead.

It says the free version keeps three reminders

Everything is open for the first fourteen days. After that the free version keeps three reminders at a time. Mark one done or delete it to set another, or unlock Encore Full in Settings, a single purchase that removes the limit for good.

I bought Encore Full on another phone

Open Settings and tap Restore a purchase while signed in to Google Play with the account that bought it. If the card says the store is not ready, check the connection and try again in a moment.

I set a time in the past by mistake

A time that has already passed today is taken to mean tomorrow when you type it. If you pick a past time from the date and time picker, the first call comes straight away. Open the reminder and tap Later or a quick time to move it.

Can I move my reminders to a new phone?

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Encore's data is included and comes back when you set up the new phone from that backup. There is no account of Encore's own to sign in to; Encore Full is tied to your Google account and can be restored from Settings.

I want to start over

Clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Encore → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall and reinstall. Encore Full can be restored afterwards.
