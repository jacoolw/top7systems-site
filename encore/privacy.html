Encore: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") built Encore, a reminder app for Android. It was designed to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains how Encore handles information. The short version: Encore has no account, collects no personal information, shows no ads and tracks no one. Your reminders live on your phone.

No Data Collected or Shared by Us

Encore does not send any data about you to us. We run no server for the app, keep no user database and include no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software. This matches the Data safety section on the app's Google Play page: no data collected, no data shared.

Data That Stays on the Device

Everything you create in Encore is stored in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Reminders: their titles, notes, times, repeat rules, encore settings, and whether and when they were marked done.
  • Call history: when each call rang and how late it was, kept for thirty days so the Reliability screen can show the last three.
  • Settings: your defaults, theme, alert choices, whether setup was completed, and the date the app was first run, which decides the length of the free trial.

None of it leaves the phone. Other apps cannot read it.

Internet Use

Encore does not request permission to use the internet for its own features. The only network activity associated with the app is Google Play Billing, described below, which is carried out by Google Play on your phone when you choose to buy or restore Encore Full. Searching, setting and ringing reminders need no connection at all.

Google Services

  • Google Play and Google Play Billing: distribution of the app and its updates, and processing of the optional Encore Full purchase and its restoration. Ownership is read from Google Play's records on your phone; we keep no purchase database and never receive your payment details.
  • Crash reports and statistics: if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may show us anonymised crash reports and install figures in aggregate. Google controls this, and you can turn it off in your Google account settings.

These are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Encore asks for the permissions a reminder needs and nothing else:

  • Notifications, to show each call.
  • Alarms and reminders, so calls ring at the exact minute. Android asks you to allow this on its own settings page.
  • Full-screen alerts, optional, so the encore can show over the lock screen. It is off until you turn it on.
  • Run at start-up, so reminders are set again after the phone restarts.

Encore asks for no access to your location, contacts, calendar, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Backups and New Devices

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Encore's data is included in your device backup like any app's data, and moves to a new phone when you restore from it. Those backups are made, encrypted and managed by Google under your control and your Google account's settings; they are not sent to us.

Purchases

Encore is free to use, with every feature open for the first fourteen days and three reminders at a time after that. A single one-time purchase, Encore Full, removes the limit. The purchase is processed entirely by Google through Google Play. There is no subscription.

Children

Encore is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, because the app collects none.

Data Deletion

Because data is stored only on your phone, you are in control of it: delete individual reminders in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. The Encore Full purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored from inside the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@top7systems.net. Top 7 Systems is based in Australia.
