The idea

A reminder you can miss once, not twice.

Most reminders ring once and give up. Encore rings, waits, and rings again, at the interval you chose, until you say it is done. Every call draws another ring around the reminder, so you can see how long it has been asking.

First call

It rings on time

Type "Call the plumber at 2:30" or tap a time from the grid. At 2:30 it rings, with Done, Snooze and Later right there in the notification.

The encore

It asks again, until you answer

Missed it? Five minutes later it rings again, and again, up to the number of calls you set or until it is done. Over the lock screen if you like.

Reliability

It checks your phone for you

Android has three switches that can stop any reminder ringing. The Reliability screen shows each one, fixes it in a tap, and rings a test call so you can see it work.

How it works

Four taps, then it looks after itself.

01

Say when

"In 20 min", "at 3pm", "tomorrow 9am", or one tap on the quick-time grid. Encore reads the time out of what you typed.

02

Choose the encore

Every 1, 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, up to ten calls or until done. The defaults are five minutes and five calls.

03

Let it ring

Each call is a notification with Done, Snooze 10 and Later. Turn on full-screen alerts and the encore fills the lock screen.

04

Mark it done

Done ends the calls and writes it into your history, with how many calls it took. Undo is there for a few seconds if you tap too soon.

Questions

Good to know

Why does it ask for the Alarms and reminders permission?

Android only lets an app ring at an exact minute when you allow it. Without that switch, a call can arrive up to ten minutes late. Encore asks once, during setup, and the Reliability screen shows whether it is on.

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no server. Your reminders live in the app's private storage on your phone. See the privacy policy.

What happens if I never mark it done?

After the number of calls you set (five by default) Encore goes quiet and leaves the reminder on the home screen, still waiting. Set it to Until done and it keeps calling.

Will it ring when the phone is locked?

Yes. Calls use Android's exact alarms, which fire with the screen off. Turn on full-screen alerts in Settings and the second call onwards fills the lock screen with the encore.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 and newer. It follows your system dark mode and can take its colours from your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer.

Is Encore Full a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase through Google Play. Restore it on a new phone from Settings with the same Google account.
