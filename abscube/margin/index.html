Growth figures are this app's own forecasts of published ABS indices out to the chosen horizon, not ABS projections. The model holds your volumes and cost mix constant and assumes index movements reach your business one for one, so treat it as a budgeting aid rather than a forecast of your business. CPI-based lines measure household prices, which stand in for business costs rather than matching them: rents are residential, insurance is household and motor, and electricity is distorted by household rebates a business does not receive. Statutory on-costs such as the superannuation guarantee are not modelled — check the current rate with the ATO.
