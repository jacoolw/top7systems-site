The answer, in order-ready units

Not just cubic yards

A volume on its own does not tell you what to buy. ConcreteCalc turns it into the numbers you quote down the phone.

  • Volume in cubic yards, cubic feet and cubic metres at once.
  • Bags needed for every bag size you have enabled — 80 lb, 60 lb and 40 lb, or 40, 30, 25 and 20 kg.
  • Truck loads against your local ready-mix capacity, 10 yd³ or 7.6 m³ by default.
  • Estimated cost from your supplier's rate per yard or metre, plus a flat delivery fee.
  • Project totals — add several pours together and share a plain-text summary with the supplier or client.

Questions

Before you download

How accurate are the calculations?

The formulas use standard geometry — the same maths your ready-mix supplier uses — and every one of them is published on the support page with a worked example, so you can check the numbers yourself. Accuracy then comes down to how precisely you measure the site, which is what the waste factor is there to absorb.

Should I use bags or order ready-mix?

As a general rule, bags are practical for jobs under 1 cubic yard (0.75 m³). Anything larger and ready-mix is faster, cheaper per unit, and gives a more consistent result. The Reference tab goes into more detail.

What waste factor should I use?

7% is the standard recommendation and the app's default. Use 5% for simple flat slabs with tight formwork, 10% for footings poured against earth or complex shapes, and 15% for pumped or irregular pours. When in doubt, go higher.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. There is no account to create and no internet connection required, so it works on site, in a basement, or anywhere you have your phone.

Can I switch between metric and imperial?

Yes, in Settings. The app defaults to the system used in your country. Bag sizes and truck capacity switch with it — 40/60/80 lb bags and 10 yd³ trucks in imperial, 20/25/30/40 kg bags and 7.6 m³ trucks in metric — and you can customise both.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your project, cost settings and preferences are saved on your device and are never sent to any server. See the privacy policy.
