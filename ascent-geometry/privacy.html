Ascent: Geometry Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") built Ascent: Geometry, a high school Geometry game for students aged 13 and up. We designed it to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information, and the short version is simple: Ascent: Geometry does not collect your personal information, show ads, or track anyone.

Data Not Collected

Ascent: Geometry does not collect any data from you. It collects no personal information and no usage or device data, on our servers or anywhere else. This matches the "Data Not Collected" privacy label shown on the app's App Store product page. The app has no user accounts and no analytics, and it sends nothing about you to us or to any third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

Ascent: Geometry does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Birth dates, photos, audio, or location
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or IP-based profiles
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, or tracking information
  • Any personal information from teens or adults

We have no user accounts and no server that receives your data, because the app does not send it anywhere.

Data That Stays on the Device

Everything Ascent: Geometry creates stays on your device. This includes:

  • Climber profiles: if more than one person uses the app, each climber has their own profile (a name) and their own climb. Profiles are not accounts; they are simply separate save slots on your device.
  • Learning progress: which skills each climber has practiced and mastered, altitude, landmarks reached, streaks, expedition patches, the Expedition Log, Route Test results, and Weather Window results.
  • Placement results: the optional check at the start (the approach) runs entirely on the device and is used only to choose a starting point on the route.
  • Settings and preferences: the sound and haptics switches and the on or off state of optional features.

This information is stored locally on the device and is used only to run the app and adapt the learning experience. This version of the app does not sync between devices, so nothing leaves the device at all; if cloud sync is added in a future version it will use your own private iCloud account, and this policy will be updated first.

Purchases

Ascent: Geometry is free to start, and a single one-time purchase (the Full Ascent) opens the whole mountain. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. Ownership is read from Apple's own transaction records on your device, so we run no purchase server and keep no purchase database. The unlock supports Family Sharing and Ask to Buy, and there is no subscription.

Age Checks Required by Law

Some US states require every app to ask the device which age range its user falls into, through Apple's Declared Age Range feature. Where Apple marks an account as covered by such a law, Ascent: Geometry asks the system once, about once a year. Nothing in the app is gated by age, so the answer changes nothing. The answer is never stored, never sent to us, and never shared; the device keeps only the date of the last check so it does not ask again at every launch.

No Ads and No Tracking

Ascent: Geometry contains no advertising and no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software. We do not build profiles of users, and we do not sell or share data, because we do not collect it in the first place.

Sharing You Control

Reaching the summit earns a summit certificate, rendered on your device. It is shared only when you choose to share it, using the standard iOS share sheet, and it goes only where you send it. The same is true of a copied Weather Window result. Nothing is posted or transmitted automatically.

Apple Services We Use

  • Apple App Store (StoreKit): processing of the optional Full Ascent purchase, restores, and offer-code redemption.
  • Apple Declared Age Range: the on-device age check described above, only where a law requires it.

We do not use any non-Apple analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and the app contains no third-party code.

Young People's Privacy

Ascent: Geometry is designed for students roughly 13 and up, and many of its users are minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, minors included. There is no behavioral advertising, no third-party data sharing, and no social features that expose a learner to other users. If you believe any personal information has somehow reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Deletion

Because data is stored on your device, you are in control of it:

  • Deleting a climber profile inside the app removes that climber's progress.
  • Deleting Ascent: Geometry from the device removes all of the app's local data.
  • The Full Ascent purchase is tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on a new device from inside the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us
