Questions

Good things to know.

Is Lyrebird official?

No. Lyrebird is an independent study aid made by Top 7 Systems. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs. The Department says everything you need to pass is in its free booklet, and that is true. Lyrebird is a way of studying that booklet.

Are these the real test questions?

No. The real questions are not published, and no app has them. Lyrebird's questions are written by us in the style of the test, from the testable part of the booklet, and each one shows the passage that supports its answer.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything works offline except the purchase and the first download of a translated booklet.

What does Lyrebird know about me?

Nothing. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your progress stays on your device. The privacy policy has the details.

Why a lyrebird?

The superb lyrebird is on Australia's 10 cent coin, and its tail has sixteen feathers. The course has sixteen topics, so each feather opens as you learn one.
