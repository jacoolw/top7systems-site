Ascent for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") built Ascent, an Algebra 1 game for students aged 13 and up. We designed Ascent to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains how Ascent for Android handles information, and the short version is simple: Ascent does not collect your personal information, show ads, or track anyone.

No Data Collected or Shared

Ascent does not collect any data from you. It collects no personal information and no usage or device data, on our servers or anywhere else, and it shares nothing with anyone. This matches the Data safety section on the app's Google Play page: no data collected, no data shared. Ascent has no user accounts and no analytics, and it sends nothing about you to us or to any third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

Ascent does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Birth dates, photos, audio, or location
  • Device identifiers, the advertising ID, or IP-based profiles
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, behavioral data, or tracking information
  • Any personal information from teens or adults

We have no user accounts and no server that receives your data, because the app does not send it anywhere.

Data That Stays on the Device

Everything Ascent creates stays on your device. This includes:

  • Climber profiles: if more than one person uses the app, each climber has their own profile (a name) and their own expedition. Profiles are not accounts; they are simply separate save slots on your device.
  • Learning progress: which skills each climber has practiced and mastered, altitude, camps reached, streaks, expedition patches, Route Test results, the Expedition Log, and Weather Window results.
  • Placement results: the optional placement check at the start (the flight in) runs entirely on the device and is used only to choose a starting camp.
  • Settings and preferences: the haptics switch, and whether Ascent has already asked for a rating.

This information is stored in the app's private storage on the device and is used only to run the app and adapt the learning experience. Ascent does not sync it through any service of ours.

Backups and New Phones

If you have Android's backup turned on, Ascent's progress may be included in your device's backup to your Google account, like any app's data, and moving to a new phone with Android's transfer tool brings your climbers along. Backups are made, encrypted, and managed by Google under your control and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to them.

Purchases

Ascent is free to start, and a single one-time purchase (the Full Expedition) unlocks the whole mountain. The purchase is processed entirely by Google through Google Play; we never receive your payment details. Ownership is read from Google Play's own purchase records on your device, so we run no purchase server and keep no purchase database. There is no subscription. If your Google account needs a parent's approval for purchases, the unlock completes after they approve it in Google Play.

No Ads and No Tracking

Ascent contains no advertising and no advertising, analytics, or tracking software, and it does not use the advertising ID. We do not build profiles of users, and we do not sell or share data, because we do not collect it in the first place.

Sharing You Control

Reaching the summit earns a summit certificate, rendered on your device, and the daily Weather Window gives a result card. Either is shared only when you choose to share it, using Android's standard share sheet, and it goes only where you send it. Nothing is posted or transmitted automatically.

Google Services We Use

  • Google Play Billing: processing of the optional Full Expedition purchase and restores.
  • Google Play In-App Review: after a milestone, Ascent may show Google Play's own rating prompt, at most once per version. Any rating or review you leave is handled by Google Play.
  • Google Play: distribution of the app and its updates.

These are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use any other analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only by Google Play Billing and Google Play's rating prompt. The climb itself works fully offline.
  • Billing: to offer the Full Expedition through Google Play.
  • Vibration: for the gentle haptic taps when an answer lands, which you can turn off in Settings.

Ascent asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Young People's Privacy

Ascent is designed for students roughly 13 and up, and many of its users are minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, minors included. There is no behavioral advertising, no third-party data sharing, and no social features that expose a learner to other users. If you believe any personal information has somehow reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

Data Deletion

Because data is stored only on your device, you are in control of it:

  • Removing a climber in Ascent's Settings deletes that climber's progress.
  • Clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Ascent > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstalling Ascent removes all of its local data.
  • The Full Expedition purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored on a new device from inside the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
