Lyrebird for Android: Support

Get in touch

Email support@top7systems.net. If your message is about a question or an answer, tell us the question and the booklet page shown under it, and we will check it against the booklet. Please do not send immigration, visa or identity details. We cannot help with applications or test bookings: those are handled only by the Department of Home Affairs.

About the test

Is Lyrebird official?

No. Lyrebird is an independent study aid made by Top 7 Systems. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs. The Department says that everything you need to pass is in its free booklet, Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond, and that is true. Lyrebird is a way of studying that booklet. The official booklet and the official practice test are free at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au.

Are these the real test questions?

No. The real questions are not published, and no app has them. Lyrebird's questions are written by us in the style of the test, from the testable part of the booklet. Every answer shows the booklet page and the booklet's own words, so you can check it yourself.

What do I need to pass?

The test has 20 multiple-choice questions and you have 45 minutes. To pass you need at least 15 correct answers, and all 5 questions about Australian values must be right. The test is in English.

Why did I fail a mock test with 17 out of 20?

Because one of the five values questions was wrong. That is how the real test is marked, and it is the most common surprise on test day. The result screen shows which questions you missed, and the Values trainer on the Practise tab repeats each values question until you have answered it correctly three times in a row.

When should I book my test?

The Today tab shows three checks: most of the course learned, the values questions secure, and your last three mock tests passed. When all three are ticked you are well prepared. This is our guide, not a promise of a result.

The booklet and languages

The test is in English. Why does Lyrebird offer other languages?

So you can understand a page before you learn it in English. The Department of Home Affairs publishes the booklet in 40 languages, and the translations match the English booklet page for page. Choose your language once and the booklet opens at the same page in your language, with English one tap away.

A translated booklet will not download

Translations are downloaded from the Department's website the first time you choose a language, and each is between 12 and 27 MB. Check your connection and tap Try again. If it still fails, tap Open the official page to read it in your browser, or keep studying in English. Once a translation is saved it works without the internet.

How do I remove a downloaded translation?

Open the booklet, tap the globe, choose Change language, then tap the bin beside a language that says "Downloaded".

Why is the picture of the Coat of Arms missing from the English booklet?

The booklet is published under a Creative Commons licence that does not cover the Commonwealth Coat of Arms, so the copy inside the app leaves that one picture out. The words about it are all there. You can see the picture in the official booklet.

How do I zoom in on the booklet?

Pinch with two fingers, or tap twice quickly. Drag to move around the page, and tap twice again to see the whole page.

Read-aloud says it needs an English voice, or the voice sounds flat

Lyrebird reads with the text-to-speech voices installed on your phone, and only uses ones that work without the internet. It looks for an Australian English voice first, then British, then any English. To add or change voices, open your phone's Settings and search for Text-to-speech, open the settings of the speech engine shown there, choose Install voice data and download English (Australia). You can change the reading speed in Lyrebird's own settings.

Purchases

Is there a subscription?

No. The full course is one payment, once. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

What is free?

All 81 lessons, read-aloud, the glossary, the booklet in 40 languages, practice questions for Part 1, your first 10 values questions and one mock test. The full course adds every practice question, the full values trainer, unlimited mock tests and the readiness check.

I paid, but the full course is locked

Tap the gear on the Today tab and choose Restore a purchase, while signed in to Google Play with the same Google account that made the purchase. This is also how you unlock the app on a new phone or a tablet. If you paid with cash or another slow payment method, the course unlocks by itself when the payment clears.

I bought the course on an iPhone. Is it unlocked on Android?

No. The App Store and Google Play are separate shops, and neither lets us see what was bought in the other, so a purchase unlocks the app only on the platform where it was made.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds are too. See Google Play's refund help, or email us and we will help where we can.

Your progress

Where is my progress kept?

On your device. There is no account, so progress does not sync between two devices. If you have Android backup turned on it is included in that backup, in your own Google account, so it comes with you when you set up a new phone.

I left the app in the middle of a mock test

The test is still there when you come back, and its clock has kept running, as it would on test day. If the 45 minutes ran out while the app was closed, that test is dropped and not marked.

How do I start again?

Tap the gear on the Today tab and choose Reset my progress. Your answers, lessons read and mock tests are cleared. Your purchase is not affected.

I think an answer is wrong

Under every answer is the passage from the booklet, and Open this page shows the page it comes from. If you still think we have it wrong, email us the question. Every question was checked against the booklet before release, and we fix mistakes quickly.
