Lyrebird for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") built Lyrebird, a study course for the Australian citizenship test. This page is about the Android app; the iPhone and iPad app has its own page. It was designed to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains how Lyrebird handles information. The short version: Lyrebird has no account, collects no personal information, shows no ads and tracks no one. Your progress lives on your device.

No Data Collected or Shared by Us

Lyrebird does not send any data about you to us. We run no server for the app, keep no user database and include no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software. This matches the Data safety section on the app's Google Play page: no data collected, no data shared.

Data That Stays on the Device

Everything Lyrebird remembers is stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your progress: which lessons you have read, how you answered each practice question, and the results of your mock tests.
  • Your choices: your test date if you enter one, your booklet language and your reading speed.
  • Translated booklets: any official translation you have chosen to download.
  • Purchase status: whether the full course is unlocked, so the app opens without waiting for Google Play.

If you have Android backup turned on, your progress and your choices are included in the backup that Android keeps in your own Google account, so they come with you to a new phone. Translated booklets and purchase status are not backed up. We cannot see any of it. You can clear your progress at any time in Settings, under Reset my progress, and uninstalling the app removes everything from the device.

Internet Use

Lyrebird works without the internet. It only uses the internet for these things:

  • Purchases: buying or restoring the full course goes through Google Play. We never receive your payment details.
  • Translated booklets: if you choose a booklet language other than English, the app downloads that official translation from immi.homeaffairs.gov.au, a website of the Australian Government's Department of Home Affairs. As with any download, that website can see your device's internet address. Lyrebird sends it nothing else, and nothing about you or your progress. This only happens when you choose a language.
  • Ratings: after a passed mock test the app may ask Google Play to show its own rating box. Whether you rate is between you and Google Play. The app is not told and keeps no record.
  • Links you tap: the links to the official booklet page, the official practice test and these web pages open in your browser.

Reading Aloud

Lessons and questions are read aloud by the text-to-speech voices installed on your phone or tablet. Lyrebird only uses voices that work without the internet, so the speech is made on the device. No text or audio is sent anywhere, and the app does not use the microphone.

Purchases

The full course is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Google Play. There is no subscription. Google's handling of purchase data is described in Google's Privacy Policy. A purchase made on Android belongs to your Google account. It does not carry over to the iPhone app, and a purchase made there does not carry over to Android, because the two stores are separate.

Not a Government Service

Lyrebird is an independent study aid. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Australian Government or the Department of Home Affairs, and it never asks for your immigration, visa or identity details. Your citizenship application and your test booking are made only with the Department, at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au.

Children's Privacy

Lyrebird does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no accounts, no social features and no way to contact other people.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
