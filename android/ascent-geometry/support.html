Ascent: Geometry for Android: Support

Need Help?

We are here to help you get the most out of Ascent: Geometry. Check the common questions below, or reach out to us directly. To see what the app covers and how the climb works, visit the Ascent: Geometry for Android page. Looking for the Algebra 1 app? Its support page is here.

Unlocking the Full Course

Ascent: Geometry is free to download and free to play through Hirli: the first three legs, which cover the tools of geometry, reasoning and proof, and parallel lines. To climb past Hirli, you unlock the Full Ascent: a single one-time purchase, made inside the app through Google Play. There is no subscription, and one unlock covers every climber profile on the device. You buy it in the app, not on this website.

1

Open the unlock screen

There are two ways in. On the mountain screen, tap the Full Ascent card that appears once you reach Hirli. Or open Settings (the gear at the top right) and choose For grown-ups. Both lead to the same place.

2

Pass the grown-ups check

A quick "For a grown-up" arithmetic question keeps the purchase in adult hands. Answer it to continue. If it is answered wrong, the app simply asks a grown-up to help, and nothing is charged.

3

Tap Unlock and pay

The Unlock button shows the one-time price for your country. Tap it, and Google Play's own purchase sheet appears. Confirm with the payment method on your Google account, and your fingerprint or password if you have Google Play ask for one. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card details.

4

Keep climbing

The rest of the mountain opens right away, with no restart. If your Google account needs a parent's approval for purchases (with Google Family Link), the app shows that the purchase was sent for approval, and the unlock activates automatically once they approve.

Already bought it? On a new device, or after reinstalling, open the same screen and tap Bought it already? Restore purchases. while signed in to Google Play with the Google account that made the purchase. The unlock comes back at no extra charge.

If the unlock screen says Google Play isn't answering, the app could not reach Google Play to fetch the price. Check the connection, then tap Try again.

Redeeming an Unlock Code

If you have a Google Play promo code for the Full Ascent, such as a launch promo we shared, you can redeem it to unlock the Full Ascent. A promotional code may make the unlock free.

1

Open the unlock screen

Reach it the same way as a purchase: the Full Ascent card on the mountain screen, or Settings → For grown-ups. Pass the quick grown-ups check to continue.

2

Tap "Have a code? Redeem it here."

You will find this link below the Unlock and restore options on the unlock screen. It opens Google Play's redeem page. You can also redeem from the Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Redeem code.

3

Enter your code

Type or paste your code into Google Play and confirm. Then come back to Ascent: Geometry: it checks with Google Play as you return, and the Full Ascent unlocks as soon as the code is accepted.

Codes are redeemed through your Google account, so make sure Google Play is signed in to the account you want the unlock tied to. Each code can be used once.

Calling the Hut for Help

Every problem carries a radio to the Hörnli Hut. When you are stuck, you call the hut and they talk you through it, one small step at a time, so you still do the thinking. Calling is never punished: no altitude is lost and nothing is marked against you.

1

Tap the Radio button

It is the radio-waves icon at the top of every problem screen. After a miss, the banner under the problem also offers Call the hut. The hut's panel slides up over your problem.

2

Tap Call the hut

The first reply is a gentle nudge: a hint that points you back at the right part of the figure or the statement without giving anything away.

3

Ask for more if you need it

Tap Ask for more to climb the hint ladder. There are four levels in all, from that first nudge up to the full worked idea. Stop at whichever level gets you moving again; the earlier rungs never state the answer.

4

Close the radio and answer

Tap Close to put the radio down and finish the problem yourself. After any answer, right or wrong, you can also tap Why? to see the worked reasoning step by step.

Reading a Figure

Every figure in Ascent: Geometry is drawn to the measures it states, so what you see is what the question means. On a phone the figure can get small once the keypad is up, so every figure has a magnifier.

1

Tap the magnifier

It is the small magnifying-glass button in the corner of the figure; a tap anywhere on the figure works too. The figure opens full screen, with the question above it.

2

Read the marks

Tick marks show congruent segments, arcs show congruent angles, a small square marks a right angle, arrowheads on two lines mean they are parallel, and dashed edges are the hidden edges of a solid. Every labeled length and angle is true to the drawing.

3

Tap Close to answer

The figure closes and the keypad is where you left it.

Building a Proof

Two-column proofs are built by tapping, not typing. The table sits in the working card, and the statements and reasons you can use sit in the keypad's place below it, so everything stays in reach.

1

Tap a statement, then its reason

Choose a statement chip (grey) and the reason that justifies it (teal). The pair appears as the next row of the proof. A row you did not mean can be removed by tapping it in the table.

2

Order is up to you

Any order that respects what each step depends on is a valid proof. If a row cites something not yet established, the app names that row when you check and brings it into view, so you know exactly where the argument broke. If only that row's reason is wrong, the next reason you tap replaces it.

3

Watch for circular reasoning

Citing CPCTC before the triangles are congruent, or citing the very theorem you are proving, is caught and explained rather than just marked wrong.

Proofs get harder as you master them: at first only the reasons are missing, then the pairs need ordering, and finally you build the whole proof from banks that include distractors.

Using the Scratchpad

Real climbers do rough work, and Ascent: Geometry gives you room for it. The scratchpad is private scrap paper: the app never reads or grades what you put on it.

1

Tap the Scratchpad button

It is the pencil icon at the top of every problem screen, Route Tests included. The scratchpad opens as a sheet over your problem.

2

Choose Draw or Type

Draw is for working by hand with a finger or a stylus, with a pen, a highlighter, and an eraser to choose from. Type is for quick rough notes on the keypad. Switch between them any time with the picker at the top.

3

Work it out, then come back

Sketch the figure again, write the equation, try a value, whatever helps. Tap Clear to wipe the page and start over, and Done to return to the problem and enter your answer.

The scratchpad keeps your drawing until the next problem, then wipes clean like real scrap paper. Nothing you write there is stored or seen by anyone.

Common Questions

Who is Ascent: Geometry for?

Anyone taking or retaking high school Geometry, roughly ages 13 and up. Sixty-seven skills across twelve legs cover a full US Geometry course, from angle pairs and first proofs to circles, coordinates, area, and volume. Reaching the summit means finishing the course. For Algebra 1 we make Ascent: Algebra 1, and for ages 8 to 11 Numina, our arithmetic game for iPhone and iPad.

I already know some geometry. Do I have to start at the bottom?

No. When a climber starts, the app offers Find my starting point: a short, optional placement check of up to six questions that can move you up the approach, as high as the Hörnli Hut, with everything below credited in full. The ridge above the hut is always climbed. If you are new to geometry, tap Start from the beginning and begin in Zermatt. The check ends kindly at the first miss, so it never becomes a test you can fail.

What does it cost?

Ascent: Geometry is free through Hirli: the tools of geometry, reasoning and proof, and parallel lines. A single one-time purchase, the Full Ascent, opens the rest of the mountain from the Hörnli Hut to the summit. There is no subscription, no ads, and nothing else to buy.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

The Full Ascent is tied to your Google account. On a new device, open the unlock screen and tap Bought it already? Restore purchases., and make sure Google Play is signed in to the same Google account that bought it. The unlock also works for every climber profile on the device.

Can a parent approve the purchase?

Yes. If a teen's Google account needs a parent's approval for purchases (with Google Family Link), they can request the Full Ascent, the app shows it was sent for approval, and it unlocks automatically once the parent approves. One purchase covers every climber on the device. Google Play's Family Library does not share in-app purchases, so a purchase on one family member's account does not unlock another's.

I'm stuck on a problem. What do I do?

Call the hut. The radio button is on every problem and is never punished: it gives four levels of help, from a small nudge to the full worked idea. Every skill also has a lesson card you can reread from the Field Guide, with a worked example, and the scratchpad gives you space for rough work with your finger or a stylus.

Do I need a calculator for the trigonometry?

No. When a problem needs the value of a sine, cosine, or tangent, the problem prints the values for that angle. Choosing the right ratio and setting up the equation is the skill being practiced, so that part stays yours. Answers with roots and π are typed exactly, such as 11√3 or 7π, and any correctly simplified form counts.

How do I type an answer like a coordinate or a square root?

The keypad changes with the question. Ordered pairs get a comma key, so (7, 3) is typed as it reads. Roots and π have their own keys when a question can take them, and degree signs are added for you when the question asks for an angle. The answer line names what it wants, such as "x =" or "m∠P =", so you only type the value.

What are Route Tests and the Final Test?

A test for every leg of the route, plus the Final Test for the whole mountain, open from day one under the checklist button on the mountain screen. There is no radio, one try per problem, and nothing on the mountain moves: a test only tells you where you stand. Tests for the paid legs open with the Full Ascent.

Why am I being asked to review old skills?

That is the acclimatization system doing its job. The app schedules spaced reviews of earlier skills and mixes them into climbs, because coming back to something right before you would forget it is what makes it stick. Altitude means a skill is truly owned, not just seen once.

What is the Weather Window?

A short daily challenge of five problems, separate from the main climb, with its own streak. It is the same for every climber at your point on the route on a given day, so you can compare results. It appears after your first climb, and missing a day never takes away altitude or patches.

Can more than one person use the app?

Yes. Ascent: Geometry supports multiple climbers on one device, each with their own climb: separate progress, altitude, streaks, and patches. Switch climbers from the profile button on the mountain screen. Profiles are not accounts and need no sign-up, and one Full Ascent unlock covers all of them.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole app works offline. An internet connection is only used for the Google Play purchase itself and Google Play's rating prompt.

Does it sync between my phone and tablet?

No. Progress stays on the device you climb on, so each phone or tablet keeps its own climbers. If you have Android's backup turned on, it includes the app's progress, and moving to a new phone with Android's transfer tool brings your climbers along.

Can I turn the sounds off?

Yes. Settings has a Sounds switch next to Haptics. The sounds stay quiet whenever the phone is set to silent or vibrate, and they play over any music you have on without stopping it.

Where is my summit certificate?

After you summit, the certificate is yours to revisit any time from Settings, and you can share it with Android's standard share sheet. Your expedition patches live in the patch gallery, also in Settings, and the Expedition Log there lists every skill on the route and where you stand with it.

The app crashed or is not responding

Make sure you are running the latest version of Ascent: Geometry from Google Play and that your device's Android version is up to date (the app needs Android 8.0 or later). Try closing the app from the recent apps screen and reopening it, and if needed restart the device. A climb interrupted mid-problem is saved: it comes back where you were, or is banked the next time you open the app. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model and a short description of what you were doing when it happened.
