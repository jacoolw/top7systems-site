Questions

Good things to know.

Is Robin official?

No. Robin is an independent study course made by Top 7 Systems. It is not connected with, approved by or endorsed by the UK Government, the Home Office or the handbook's publisher. It teaches the facts in the handbook in its own words and tells you where each one is in the handbook.

Are these the real test questions?

No. The real questions are not published, and no app has them. Robin's questions are written by us in the four formats the test uses, from the facts in the handbook. Every answer has its reason and the handbook section it comes from, so you can check it yourself.

What do I need to pass?

At least 18 correct answers out of 24 (75%), within 45 minutes. The test is in English and costs £50 each time. You book it at GOV.UK.

The test is in English. Why Chinese?

So you can understand an answer before you learn it in English. With Traditional Chinese turned on, explanations, lesson summaries and glossary words also appear in Chinese. Questions and answers stay in English, as in the test. 閱讀繁體中文版本.

Is there a subscription?

No. The full course is one payment of £9.99 in the UK, once. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews. Robin has no adverts, no account and no tracking, and it works offline. The privacy policy has the details.
