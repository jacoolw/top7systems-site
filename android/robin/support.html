Robin for Android: Support

Get in touch

Email support@top7systems.net. If your message is about a question or an answer, tap Think this is wrong? Tell us under its explanation, or tell us the question's number, and we will check it against the handbook. Please do not send immigration, visa or identity details. We cannot help with applications or test bookings: those are handled only by the Home Office, through GOV.UK.

About the test

Is Robin official?

No. Robin is an independent study course made by Top 7 Systems. It is not connected with, approved by or endorsed by the UK Government, the Home Office or the publisher of the official handbook. The test is based on the official handbook, Life in the United Kingdom: A Guide for New Residents (3rd edition). Robin teaches the facts in that handbook in its own words and tells you where each one is in the handbook.

Are these the real test questions?

No. The real questions are not published, and no app has them. Robin's questions are written by us in the four formats the test uses, from the facts in the handbook. Every answer has its reason and the handbook section it comes from, so you can check it yourself.

What do I need to pass?

The test has 24 questions and you have 45 minutes. To pass you need at least 18 correct answers (75%). It is taken on a computer at an approved test centre, in English, and costs £50 each time. You book it at GOV.UK, at least three days ahead.

What is a Handbook note?

The test follows the handbook, and in a few places the handbook is out of date. Where that matters, Robin shows a Handbook note: what the handbook says, what is true today, and what to answer in the test. In the test, the handbook's answer is the right one.

When should I book my test?

The Today tab shows three checks: most of the course learned, the dates and numbers secure, and your last three mock tests passed. When all three are ticked you are well prepared. This is our guide, not a promise of a result.

Help in Chinese and reading aloud

The test is in English. Why does Robin offer Chinese?

So you can understand an answer before you learn it in English. Questions and answers stay in English, as in the test. With Traditional Chinese turned on, each explanation, each lesson's summary and key facts, and each glossary word also appear in Chinese. Turn it on or off in Settings.

The reading voice sounds flat

Robin reads with the text-to-speech voices installed on your phone, and only uses ones that work without the internet. It looks for a British English voice first, then any English. To add or change voices, open your phone's Settings and search for Text-to-speech, open the settings of the speech engine shown there, choose Install voice data and download an English (United Kingdom) voice. You can change the reading speed in Robin's own settings.

Purchases

Is there a subscription?

No. The full course is one payment, once. There is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

What is free?

Every lesson, read-aloud, the glossary and the timeline, practice questions for chapters 1 and 2 and the first history topic, and one mock test. The full course adds every practice question, the full dates and numbers trainer, the timeline challenge and unlimited mock tests.

I paid, but the full course is locked

Tap the gear on the Today tab and choose Restore a purchase, while signed in to Google Play with the same Google account that made the purchase. This is also how you unlock the app on a new phone or a tablet. If you paid with cash or another slow payment method, the course unlocks by itself when the payment clears.

I bought the course on an iPhone. Is it unlocked on Android?

No. The App Store and Google Play are separate shops, and neither lets us see what was bought in the other, so a purchase unlocks the app only on the platform where it was made.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds are too. See Google Play's refund help, or email us and we will help where we can.

Your progress

Where is my progress kept?

On your device. There is no account, so progress does not sync between two devices. If you have Android backup turned on it is included in that backup, in your own Google account, so it comes with you when you set up a new phone.

How do I start again?

Tap the gear on the Today tab and choose Reset all progress. Your answers, lessons read and mock tests are cleared. Your purchase is not affected.

I think an answer is wrong

Under every explanation is the handbook section it comes from. If you still think we have it wrong, tap Think this is wrong? Tell us there, or email us the question's number. We check every report against the handbook and fix mistakes quickly.
