Hammock for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") built Hammock, a downtime organiser for Android. It was designed to be private by default. This Privacy Policy explains how Hammock handles information. The short version: Hammock has no account, collects no personal information, shows no ads and tracks no one. Your lists live on your phone.

No Data Collected or Shared by Us

Hammock does not send any data about you to us. We run no server for the app, keep no user database and use no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking software. This matches the Data safety section on the app's Google Play page: no data collected, no data shared with us.

Data That Stays on the Device

Everything you create in Hammock is stored in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your library: the items you save (titles, creators, years, cover image links, descriptions, links), their status, progress, ratings, notes and tags.
  • Lists and categories: the lists you make and any categories you rename, hide or add.
  • Logbook: the dates you started, finished or dropped things.
  • Settings: theme, dark mode, layout and whether onboarding was shown.

Hammock does not sync this information through any service of ours. Cover images fetched from catalogues are cached on the device so they work offline.

Internet Use You Trigger

Hammock only uses the internet when you ask it to do one of these things:

  • Searching a catalogue: the words you type are sent to the catalogues being searched so they can return matches: Open Library (books), Wikipedia (films), TVmaze (series), Apple's public iTunes Search catalogue (podcasts and music) and the Steam store (games). If the app has been configured with keys for them, The Movie Database (TMDB) and IGDB are searched as well. Each service receives your search text and the usual connection details such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy.
  • Loading covers: cover images are downloaded from the same catalogues or from the page you shared.
  • Sharing a link to Hammock: the page at that link is fetched once to read its title, description and image. The request goes to that website, not to us. The title is then looked up in the matching catalogue above, so a film or game gets its poster. IMDb does not let apps read its pages, so for an IMDb link only the title's IMDb number is sent to Wikidata, which records the film or series it belongs to.
  • Opening a link: tapping Open link hands the address to your browser.

Nothing is sent in the background, and nothing is sent if you only add items by hand.

Google Services We Use

  • Google Play Billing: processing of the optional Hammock Plus purchase and restores. Ownership is read from Google Play's purchase records on your device; we keep no purchase database.
  • Google Play services fonts: Hammock's typefaces are fetched through Google Play's font provider the first time they are needed. On devices without Google Play services the system font is used instead.
  • Google Play: distribution of the app and its updates.

These are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Backups and New Phones

If Android's backup is turned on, Hammock's data may be included in your device backup to your Google account, like any app's data, and may move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool. Those backups are made, encrypted and managed by Google under your control. Hammock also lets you export your own backup file to a location you choose; that file is yours and is not sent to us.

Sharing You Control

Hammock can render a list as a picture for you to post. The picture is created on your device and shared only when you choose to, through Android's standard share sheet, to the app you pick. Nothing is posted or transmitted automatically, and Hammock has no social features of its own.

Purchases

Hammock is free to use. A single one-time purchase, Hammock Plus, unlocks extra themes, statistics, custom categories and the widget. The purchase is processed entirely by Google through Google Play; we never receive your payment details. There is no subscription.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only for catalogue searches, cover images, reading pages you share, fonts and Google Play Billing.
  • Vibration: gentle haptic taps when you change an item's status.

Hammock asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. Backup files are written only to the location you choose through Android's file picker.

Children

Hammock is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included, because the app collects none.

Data Deletion

Because data is stored only on your device, you are in control of it: delete individual items in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Hammock > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstalling the app. The Plus purchase is tied to your Google account and can be restored from inside the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
