Three small moments

Save it. Pick it. Remember it.

Hammock is built around the three things you actually do with downtime: notice something worth getting to, choose what to do tonight, and look back on what you loved.

Capture

Save from anywhere, in one tap

Tap Share in Chrome, YouTube, Goodreads, Letterboxd or Spotify and choose Save to Hammock. It reads the page, guesses the category, grabs the cover and drops it in the Pile.

Decide

Can't decide? Pick for me

One tap chooses something from your wants, by category if you like. Pick again, or start it. There's a home-screen widget for the same lazy-evening moment.

Remember

A logbook of what you finished

Every start and finish is logged with your rating and notes, and the year adds up into charts by month and by category. It's yours to look back on.

Designed to be calm

Warm linen by day, dusk by night.

Serif headlines, covers that do the decorating, colour themes you can change, and a dark mode that is never pure black. Built with Material 3 for Android 8 and up.

How it works

Four habits, no homework.

01

Save

Share a link from any app, search a title, or add by hand. New finds land in the Pile until you sort them.

02

Sort

Swipe right to start, left to let go. Put things in lists that can mix books, films and games: "Rainy weekend", "Book club", "With the kids".

03

Pick

Tap Pick for me when you can't choose. Narrow it to a category or a list, or let it surprise you.

04

Finish

Track pages, episodes or hours, rate it in half stars, jot a note. The logbook keeps the story of your year.

Questions

Good to know

Does it need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no server. Everything lives in the app's private storage on your phone, and you can export a backup file whenever you like.

What does "nothing leaves your phone" mean exactly?

Hammock only talks to the internet when you ask it to search a catalogue (your search words go to that catalogue) or share a link (the page you shared is fetched to read its title and cover). There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

Is it a social network?

No. You can post any list as a picture to the apps you already use, but there is no feed, no followers and no comments to moderate.

Which catalogues does it search?

Books come from Open Library, films from Wikipedia, series from TVmaze, podcasts and music from Apple's public catalogue, and PC games from Steam. Anything else, from board games to recipes, can be added by hand or by sharing a link.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 and newer. It is built with Material 3 and follows your system dark mode and wallpaper colours if you want it to.

Is Plus a subscription?

No. It is a single purchase through Google Play. Restore it on a new phone from Settings with the same Google account.
