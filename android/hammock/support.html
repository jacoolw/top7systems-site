Hammock for Android: Guide and Support

What Hammock is for

Hammock is a downtime organiser: one calm place for every book, film, series, game, podcast and album you want to get to, plus a logbook of what you finished. There is no account and no server. Everything lives on your phone, and you can export a backup file whenever you like. This page explains each part of the app. If something is not covered, email support@top7systems.net.

Adding things

There are three ways in, and all of them take a couple of seconds.

1

Share from any app

In Chrome, YouTube, Goodreads, Letterboxd, Spotify, Steam or any other app, tap Share and choose Save to Hammock. A small sheet appears over the app you were in. Hammock reads the page, guesses the category from the site (a Goodreads link becomes a book, a Steam link a game), fills in the title and cover, and saves it to the Pile when you tap Save to the Pile. You can change the category or title first, or tap Add more details to open the full form.

2

Search every catalogue at once

Tap the + button or Search on the home screen and type a title. With Everything selected, Hammock searches books, films, series, games, podcasts and music at the same time and groups the results. Tap a category chip to narrow it down. Results already in your library appear at the top under Already in Hammock, so nothing gets added twice. Tap Add on any result; it lands in the Pile, or straight into a list if you started from one.

3

Add by hand

Tap Add manually on the search screen to type a title, creator, year, link and notes yourself. Use this for things no catalogue knows about, or for custom categories.

The Pile

The Pile is your inbox: everything you save lands there until you sort it. Swipe a row to the right to start it, or to the left to delete it (an Undo appears for a few seconds). The three-dot menu on each row offers Add to list, Start, Mark done and Delete. An item leaves the Pile as soon as it is in a list, started, or finished. The home screen shows how many things are waiting there.

Lists

Lists can mix categories: "Rainy weekend" can hold a novel, two films and a game. Create one from the Lists tab with the + button, give it a name, an icon and a colour, then add items from the Pile (menu → Add to list), from an item's page, or with the + button inside the list, which opens search with that list as the destination. The list menu lets you edit the name, icon and colour, delete the list (its items stay in your library) and Share as picture, which renders a card with the list's covers that you can post to Instagram, WhatsApp, X or anywhere else through Android's share sheet.

Pick for me

Tap the Pick for me card on the home screen when you cannot decide. Hammock chooses one thing you still want to get to. Use the chips to limit it to a category, tap Pick again for another, or Start it to mark it started. Tapping the picked item opens its page. With Hammock Plus you can also add the Pick for me widget to your home screen: it shows one random want with its cover, tapping opens it, and the shuffle button rolls again.

Progress, ratings, notes and tags

Open any item to see its page. The row of buttons sets its status: Want, Started, Done or Dropped. Marking something Done records the date and sends it to the logbook. The Progress card counts pages, episodes, hours, levels or percent: use the plus and minus buttons, or tap the card to type a current value and a total. Tap the stars to rate in half-star steps; tap the same star again to clear it. Tags are comma separated. Notes save by themselves as you type.

Library

The Library tab shows everything by category and status. Use the chips to pick a category and the tabs for Want, Started, Done and Dropped. The icon at the top right switches between a list and a poster grid, and Hammock remembers your choice.

Logbook and statistics

The clock icon on the home screen opens the Logbook: every start, finish and drop by month, with the rating you gave. The tiles at the top count what you finished this year and this month and your average rating. With Hammock Plus the logbook also shows a bar chart of finishes by month and a breakdown by category for the year.

Hammock Plus

Hammock is free to use with no limits on items or lists. Hammock Plus is a single one-time purchase made inside the app through Google Play. It is not a subscription, and it unlocks all colour themes, the statistics charts, custom categories and the home-screen widget.

1

Open Settings

Tap the gear on the home screen and scroll to the Hammock Plus card. It shows the price in your currency once Google Play has answered.

2

Tap Unlock Plus

Google Play's own purchase sheet appears. Confirm with the payment method on your Google account. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your card details.

3

Restore on another phone

On a new or reset device, open the same card and tap Restore while signed in to Google Play with the account that bought Plus. The unlock comes back at no charge.

If the card says Plus is not available for purchase yet, the app could not reach Google Play or the product is not live in your country yet. Check the connection and try again later.

Backup and restore

In Settings → Your data, tap Export backup to save a plain JSON file with all your items, lists, tags and history to a folder of your choice (Google Drive works too). Import backup merges a backup into the current library: nothing is deleted, and items that already exist (same title in the same category) are skipped. Android's own device backup may also include Hammock's data if you have it turned on.

Themes and categories

Settings → Appearance offers the warm Linen theme and its dark counterpart, Dusk, plus further colour themes (Plus) and your wallpaper's colours on Android 12 and newer. Dark mode can follow the system or be forced on or off. Settings → Categories lets you rename, recolour, re-icon and hide any built-in category, change what its progress is counted in, and, with Plus, add your own categories such as Recipes or Board games.

Data and privacy

Hammock has no account and sends nothing anywhere unless you search a catalogue or share a link. Search words go to the catalogue you searched (Open Library for books, Wikipedia for films, TVmaze for series, Apple's public catalogue for podcasts and music, Steam for games); a shared link is fetched to read its title and cover. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Troubleshooting

Search shows "Search did not work"

One of the catalogues could not be reached. Check your connection and try again; the others still answer. Some catalogues allow a limited number of searches per minute, so a short pause can help.

A cover is missing

Not every catalogue entry has artwork, and some shared pages do not publish an image. The item still works; a coloured placeholder with the first letter stands in.

"Save to Hammock" does not appear in the share sheet

It appears for links and text. Some apps share only images or files, which Hammock cannot read. Android also sometimes hides rarely used targets behind More; look there the first time.

Headlines look like a plain font

Hammock's typefaces come from Google Play's font provider. On phones without Google Play services the app falls back to the system font; everything else works the same.

I want to start over

Export a backup first if you might want it later, then clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Hammock → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall and reinstall. Plus can be restored afterwards.
