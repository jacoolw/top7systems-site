Entering measurements

Type a measurement the way you say it. Press the number, then the unit:

5 ft 6 in 3 / 8 → 5' 6-3/8"

A number after ft is taken as inches, and a fraction after in is part of that inch. Metric works the same way: 1676 mm, 1.676 m.

Bare numbers. A plain number with no unit is just a number. When you add it to a measurement it is taken in the unit set under Settings → "Bare numbers are" (inches by default), so 5 ft 6 in + 3/8 = gives 5' 6-3/8".

The display shows a strip of tape measure under the result with a red mark at your answer: 1/16" ticks, cumulative inch numbers and red foot marks, or millimetres and centimetres in metric. Long-press the display to copy the result in any unit. A sideways swipe on the display is backspace. Long-press the backspace key to clear the entry.

Math, areas and volumes

10 ft × 12 ft = → 120 sq ft
× 4 in = → 40 cu ft
yd → 1.481 cu yd

Multiplying two lengths gives an area, an area by a length gives a volume. Press any unit key or Conv on a result to convert it. Pressing a unit key twice makes it square (10 ft ft = 10 sq ft), three times cubic.

× and ÷ are evaluated before + and −, like a scientific calculator. M+ and M− accumulate results in memory; MR recalls it (long-press to clear). Every result you press = on is saved to the Tape, where you can tap it to bring it back or share the whole list. AC clears the entry; a second press clears the triangle and the memory too.

Rise, Run, Diagonal and Pitch

3 ft Rise 4 ft Run Diag → 5' 0"
6 Pitch 10 ft Run Rise → 5' 0" (6 means 6:12)
30 Deg Pitch 10 ft Run Diag → 11' 6-9/16"

Enter any two of Rise, Run, Diagonal or Pitch and press the others to read them. Press Deg before Pitch to enter degrees. The panel under the display shows all four values; tap a cell to recall it into the display for further math.

Diagonal = √(rise² + run²) Pitch (in/ft) = 12 × rise ÷ run Angle = atan(rise ÷ run)

Voice entry

Tap the microphone in the display and say a measurement: "five feet six and three eighths plus two feet". FootInch understands numbers, fractions (halves, quarters, eighths, sixteenths, thirty-seconds, sixty-fourths), feet, inches, yards, metres, centimetres and millimetres, the four operators (plus, minus, times, over) and "equals".

Recognition runs on your phone through Android's on-device speech recognizer; audio is never uploaded. That needs Android 12 or later and the offline English speech pack from Speech Services by Google. If FootInch says the pack isn't installed, open Android Settings → System → Languages → Speech (the path varies by maker; on a Samsung it is under General management → Speech), choose offline speech recognition and download English. On a phone without on-device recognition FootInch turns voice entry off rather than send audio to a cloud service.

The first tap asks for the microphone permission. If you declined it, allow it under Android Settings → Apps → FootInch → Permissions.

Solvers and their formulas

Solver fields accept measurements the way you say them: 5' 6 3/8", 5 ft 6 in, 66.375, 6-3/8, 1676 mm, 2.5'. The line under each field shows how it was read. Results update as you type, and the two key answers sit above the keyboard. Long-press a result for "Use in calculator".

Stairs (free)

Risers = ceil(total rise ÷ max riser) Riser height = total rise ÷ risers
Treads = risers − 1 Total run = treads × tread run
Stringer = √(total rise² + total run²)

Code checks use IRC R311.7.5: maximum riser 7-3/4", minimum tread 10". The comfort check is 2 × riser + tread, ideally 24–25". Always confirm against your local code.

Rafters

Run = span ÷ 2 − ridge thickness ÷ 2 Rise = run × tan(pitch)
Common length = run ÷ cos(pitch) Hip/valley = √((run × √2)² + rise²)
Jack difference = spacing ÷ cos(pitch) Plumb cut = pitch angle, seat cut = 90° − pitch angle

Lengths are line lengths from the plate line to the ridge line; the overhang tail is listed separately.

Roofing

Roof area = plan length × plan width × (1 ÷ cos(pitch)) Squares = area ÷ 100 sq ft

Concrete

Slab = L × W × T Column = π × (D ÷ 2)² × H × count 1 cu yd = 27 cu ft
Bags: 80 lb = 0.60 cu ft, 60 lb = 0.45 cu ft, 40 lb = 0.30 cu ft

Block & brick

8×8×16 block: 1.125 per sq ft, about 12 per 80 lb bag of mortar mix
Modular brick: 6.75 per sq ft, about 37 per 80 lb bag

Drywall & sheets

Sheets = net area × (1 + waste) ÷ sheet area 4×8 = 32, 4×9 = 36, 4×10 = 40, 4×12 = 48 sq ft

Board feet

Board feet = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12

Wall framing

Studs = floor(wall length ÷ spacing) + 1 + extras Plate stock = wall length × plates

Circle & arc

Radius from chord c and rise h: R = c² ÷ (8h) + h ÷ 2 Arc length = R × angle

Polygon

Miter (each cut) = 180° ÷ n Outside radius = s ÷ (2 sin(180° ÷ n)) Inside radius = s ÷ (2 tan(180° ÷ n))

Crown & compound miter (cutting flat)

Miter = atan(sin(spring) ÷ tan(corner ÷ 2)) Bevel = asin(cos(spring) × cos(corner ÷ 2))
38° spring, 90° corner → miter 31.6°, bevel 33.9°

Equal spacing

Gap at both ends: n = ceil((L − max gap) ÷ (width + max gap)), gap = (L − n × width) ÷ (n + 1)
Member at both ends: n = ceil((L + max gap) ÷ (width + max gap)), gap = (L − n × width) ÷ (n − 1)

Pitch & slope

x:12 → angle = atan(x ÷ 12) percent = tan(angle) × 100 rafter factor = 1 ÷ cos(angle) hip pitch = tan(angle) × 17 per 17

FootInch Pro and restoring purchases

FootInch Pro is a single one-time purchase, made inside the app through Google Play, that unlocks every solver. It belongs to the Google account that bought it. If you reinstall or change phones, sign in with that Google account, then open Settings → Restore Purchases (or tap any locked solver → Unlock → Restore Purchases). The unlock also comes back on its own the next time FootInch opens with that account.

There is no subscription. If you were charged more than once, request a refund from Google Play (how refunds work) and let us know.

The Pro unlock is per store: a purchase on Google Play does not unlock the iPhone app, and the other way round.

Tablets and keyboards

On a tablet the tabs move to a rail on the left and the keypad stays full size. With a hardware keyboard, digits, ., /, +, -, * and = or Enter work as you'd expect; f is feet, i inches, y yards, m metres, c centimetres, l millimetres, r Rise, u Run, d Diagonal, p Pitch, Backspace is backspace and Escape is AC.

The Keep screen awake switch in Settings stops the phone from locking while FootInch is open, for the saw horse.

FAQ

A result looks different from my handheld calculator. Check the fraction precision in Settings (1/16" by default) and the "Bare numbers are" unit. Rafter and stair results are line lengths before cuts and overhangs.

Can I enter 6-3/8 in a solver? Yes. Solver fields accept 5' 6 3/8", 5 ft 6 in, 66.375, 6-3/8, 1676 mm and 2.5'. Use the keyboard's symbol page for ' and ", or just write ft and in. The line under each field shows how it was read.

Does it work offline? Yes, entirely, voice entry included. The only network use is Google Play when you buy or restore FootInch Pro.

Which Android versions? Android 8.0 and later. Voice entry needs Android 12 or later with the offline English speech pack.

Is there a dark mode? Yes, it follows the phone's dark theme.
