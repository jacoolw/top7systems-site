FootInch for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", "us") makes FootInch, a feet-inch-fraction construction calculator. This Privacy Policy explains how FootInch for Android handles information. The short version: FootInch collects no personal information, shows no ads, and tracks no one. Your calculations never leave your phone.

No Data Collected or Shared

FootInch does not collect any data from you. It collects no personal information and no usage or device data, on our servers or anywhere else, and it shares nothing with anyone. This matches the Data safety section on the app's Google Play page: no data collected, no data shared. FootInch has no user accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting SDKs, and it sends nothing about you to us or to any third party.

Information We Do Not Collect

FootInch does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Photos, audio recordings, or location
  • Device identifiers, the advertising ID, or IP-based profiles
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, behavioral data, or tracking information
  • The measurements you type, say, or calculate

We have no user accounts and no server that receives your data, because the app does not send it anywhere.

Voice Entry and the Microphone

FootInch can take a measurement by voice ("five feet six and three eighths plus two feet"). When you tap the microphone, Android asks for permission to record audio. FootInch listens only while the microphone icon is active and only uses Android's on-device speech recognizer: the audio is turned into text on your phone and is never uploaded, stored, or sent to us. On a phone without on-device recognition (it needs Android 12 or later with the offline English speech pack installed), FootInch refuses to listen rather than fall back to a cloud service, and says so. You can decline the microphone permission and type instead; nothing else in the app changes.

Data That Stays on the Device

Everything FootInch creates stays on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • The tape: the last 200 results you pressed = on, so you can bring one back into the calculator or share it yourself.
  • Settings: measurement system, fraction precision, display style, the unit assumed for bare numbers, haptics, and the keep-screen-awake option.
  • Whether FootInch has already asked you for a rating, so it never asks twice in one version.

Sharing the tape uses Android's share sheet: the text goes only to the app you choose, and we never see it.

Backups and New Phones

If you have Android's backup turned on, FootInch's tape and settings may be included in your device's backup to your Google account, like any app's data, and moving to a new phone with Android's transfer tool brings them along. Backups are made, encrypted, and managed by Google under your control and are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We have no access to them.

Purchases

The calculator, tape, converter and stair solver are free. A single one-time purchase, FootInch Pro, unlocks every other solver. The purchase is processed entirely by Google through Google Play Billing; we never receive your payment details. Ownership is read from Google Play's own purchase records on your device, so we run no purchase server and keep no purchase database. There is no subscription. Google's handling of purchase data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Rating Requests

After you have completed a number of calculations, FootInch may use Google Play's in-app review prompt to ask for a rating, at most once per app version. The prompt is shown and handled by Google Play; FootInch does not learn whether you rated the app.

Children's Privacy

FootInch does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13. It has no user accounts, social features, or data collection of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
