Hammock for iPhone and iPad: Support

Need Help?

Hammock is a downtime organiser: one calm place for every book, film, series, game, podcast and album you want to get to, plus a logbook of what you finished. There is no account and no server, so your library lives on your device. Check the common questions below, read the full guide, or email us directly.

Common Questions

Hammock is not in the share sheet

Scroll the row of apps in the share sheet to the end and tap More, then tap Edit and add Hammock to your favourites so it shows first. Hammock appears when an app shares a link or text; apps that share only photos or files do not offer it.

Something I shared is not in the Pile

Shared items arrive the next time Hammock opens. If Hammock was already open, switch away and back. If the sheet closed before you tapped Save to the Pile, nothing was saved.

Search says "Search did not work"

A catalogue could not be reached. Check your connection and try again; the other catalogues still answer. Apple's catalogue allows a limited number of searches a minute, so a short pause can help. If a title is in no catalogue, tap Add it by hand at the end of the results.

A cover is missing or wrong

Not every catalogue entry has artwork, and some pages publish a banner rather than a poster. Open the item, tap the … button and choose Change cover: Hammock shows the artwork its catalogue has for that title, so you can pick the right one, paste an image link, or remove the cover. Edit details in the same menu fixes the title, creator and year. Without a cover, the item shows a coloured card with its first letter.

How do I restore Hammock Plus on a new device?

Open Settings (the gear on Home), tap Unlock Plus, then Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought it. Plus is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, and Family Sharing covers your family too.

Plus is not available, or the price does not show

The App Store did not answer. Check the connection and that In-App Purchases are allowed in Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions, then open Plus again.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. You are welcome to email us as well.

The widget says it is part of Hammock Plus

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets come with Plus. After buying or restoring it, open Hammock once and the widget updates within a few seconds. The Control Center button and Siri work without Plus.

Can I move my library from Hammock for Android?

Yes. Both apps read and write the same backup file. Export it in Hammock for Android (Settings > Your data > Export backup), save it to Files on your iPhone, then choose Import backup in Hammock's Settings. Nothing is deleted on import, and things you already have are skipped. The guide has step-by-step instructions.

Does my library sync between iPhone and iPad?

Not yet. Each device keeps its own library. To copy it across, export a backup on one device and import it on the other. Your device backup in iCloud also includes Hammock, so a new iPhone restored from it keeps your library.

I want to start over

Export a backup first if you might want it later, then delete the app and install it again. Restore Plus from Settings afterwards.

The app crashed or is not responding

Make sure you are running the latest version of Hammock and iOS or iPadOS. Close the app from the app switcher and open it again. If the problem continues, email us with a short description of what you were doing when it happened.
