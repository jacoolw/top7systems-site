Getting started

Hammock is a downtime organiser: one calm place for every book, film, series, game, podcast and album you want to get to, plus a logbook of what you finished. There is no account and no server. Everything lives on your iPhone or iPad.

The app has four tabs and a search button:

  • Home: Pick for me, what you are in the middle of, what is pinned to Up next, what is fresh in the Pile and what you finished recently. The clock button opens the logbook and the gear opens Settings.
  • Pile: everything saved but not sorted yet. The badge shows how many things are waiting.
  • Lists: your own collections, which can mix categories.
  • Library: everything, by category and status.
  • Search (the magnifying glass): one search across every catalogue.

Hammock runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later. On iOS 26 the tab bar and buttons use Liquid Glass; earlier versions get the classic look.

Saving from any app

The quickest way in is the share sheet. In Safari, Letterboxd, Goodreads, IMDb, Apple Podcasts, Apple Music, Apple TV, YouTube, Steam or any other app, share a page or a link and choose Hammock.

  1. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) and tap Hammock in the row of apps.
  2. A sheet opens over the app you were in. Hammock reads the page, fills in the title and cover, and guesses the category from the site: a Goodreads link becomes a book, a Steam link a game, a Spotify show a podcast. It also looks the title up in that category's catalogue, so a film saved from a review site gets its poster, director and year rather than the page's banner.
  3. Change the title or category if you like, then tap Save to the Pile.

The item appears in the Pile as soon as you next open Hammock, with a message at the bottom to open it. If you saved before the page finished loading, Hammock finishes reading it then and fills in the cover and details.

Can't see Hammock in the share sheet? Scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More, then tap Edit and add Hammock to your favourites so it shows first. Hammock appears for links and text; apps that share only photos or files do not offer it.

Searching

Tap the magnifying glass and type a title. With Everything selected, Hammock searches every catalogue at once and groups the results by category. Tap a category chip to search just that catalogue.

  • Things already in your library appear first under Already in Hammock, so nothing gets added twice. Tap one to open it.
  • Tap Add on a result (or anywhere on its row). It lands in the Pile, and a message offers to open it.
  • Searching from inside a list adds straight into that list. Searching from the Library's + starts in the category you were looking at.
  • Add manually (top right) opens a form for things no catalogue knows. Paste a link into its Link field and Hammock reads the page for the title, creator, year and cover.

The Pile

The Pile is your inbox: everything you save lands there until you sort it. An item leaves the Pile as soon as it is in a list, started, finished or dropped.

  • Swipe right on a row to start it.
  • Swipe left to delete it, or to reveal Done. Every delete shows a message with Undo for a few seconds, and Undo puts back the item with its lists, tags and history.
  • Touch and hold a row for the full menu: Start, Mark done, Pin to Up next, Add to list and Delete.

The same touch-and-hold menu works on every cover and row in the app.

An item's page

Tap any cover or row to open its page. The cover zooms open into a page with a soft backdrop made from the cover itself.

  • Status: Want, Started, Done or Dropped. Marking something Done records the date, fills its progress and sends it to the logbook. Changing your mind within ten minutes corrects the last entry rather than adding another.
  • Progress: tap + and −, or tap the card to type a current value and a total and choose what it is counted in: pages, chapters, episodes, hours, minutes, levels or percent. Adding progress to something you only wanted starts it.
  • Rating: tap the left half of a star for a half star, the right half for a whole one. Tap your current rating again to clear it.
  • Lists: the lists it is in, each removable with its ×, and Add to list.
  • Tags: separate them with commas. They save when you press Done on the keyboard or leave the field.
  • Notes: save by themselves as you type.
  • The pin button puts it on Home under Up next. The … menu edits the title, creator and year, shares the link, or deletes the item.
  • Change cover, also in the … menu, shows the artwork the catalogue has for that title so you can pick another, paste an image link, or remove the cover.

Lists

Lists can mix categories: "Rainy weekend" can hold a novel, two films and a game. Create one in the Lists tab with + and give it a name, a colour and an icon.

  • Add to a list from an item's page (Add to list), from any touch-and-hold menu, or with + inside the list, which opens search with that list as the destination.
  • Inside a list, swipe left on a row to remove it from the list. The item stays in your library.
  • The … menu edits the list, deletes it (its items stay in your library), or Share as picture.

Sharing a list as a picture

Share as picture draws a card with the list's name and up to nine covers in its colour. Tap Share to send it to Messages, Instagram, WhatsApp, Mail, Photos or anywhere else in the share sheet. The picture is made on your iPhone; nothing is posted unless you choose an app to post it to.

Pick for me

Tap the Pick for me card on Home when you cannot decide. Hammock shuffles through the things you want to get to and settles on one.

  • Tap a category chip to pick only from, say, films.
  • Pick again chooses something else; Start it marks it started and puts it under Continue.
  • Tap the pick itself to open its page.

Pick for me chooses from everything with the status Want, whether it is in the Pile or in a list.

Widgets, Control Center and Siri

Pick for me also lives outside the app. The Control Center button and Siri are free; the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets come with Hammock Plus.

Home Screen widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle.
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget.
  3. Search for Hammock, choose Pick for me in the small or medium size, and tap Add Widget.

The widget shows one thing from your wants with its cover. Tap it to open that item; tap the shuffle button to pick again without opening the app. It also changes by itself a few times a day.

Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap the widget area and add Hammock's Pick for me. It shows tonight's suggestion; tapping it opens the item.

Control Center

Open Control Center, touch and hold an empty area, tap Add a Control and search for Hammock. The Pick for me button opens Hammock straight to a fresh pick.

Siri and Shortcuts

Say "Pick something in Hammock" or "Hammock pick for me". The same action is in the Shortcuts app, so you can add it to your own shortcuts or to the Action button.

Library

The Library tab shows everything. Use the chips at the top to choose a category (or All) and the segments for Want, Started, Done and Dropped. The button at the top right switches between a list and a poster wall, and Hammock remembers your choice. + searches in the category you are looking at.

Logbook

The clock button on Home opens the logbook: every start, finish and drop by month, with the rating you gave. The tiles at the top count what you finished this year and this month, and your average rating this year.

With Hammock Plus the logbook also shows a chart of finishes for each of the last twelve months and a breakdown by category for the year. Tap any entry to open the item.

Each item counts once per month, so finishing a series and correcting its status does not inflate your numbers.

Hammock Plus

Hammock is free with no limits on items or lists. Hammock Plus is a single one-time purchase made in the app through the App Store. It is not a subscription. It unlocks all eight colour themes, the logbook charts, custom categories, and the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

  1. Open Settings (the gear on Home) and tap Unlock Plus, or tap any locked feature.
  2. The App Store's own purchase sheet appears. Confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or your password. Payment is handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.
  3. Plus unlocks at once on this device and on your other devices with the same Apple Account.

Restoring: on a new or reset device, open Plus and tap Restore Purchases. Family Sharing: if you share purchases with your family, they get Plus too. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Backups, and moving between Android and iPhone

Your library is stored on your device. iCloud Backup and computer backups of your iPhone include it like any app's data, and restoring a device backup brings it back.

Exporting a backup file

In Settings → Your data, tap Export backup and choose where to save it, for example iCloud Drive or On My iPhone in Files. The file is plain JSON with all your items, lists, tags, ratings, notes and logbook, readable by hand if you ever need to.

Importing a backup

Tap Import backup and choose the file. Importing merges: nothing is deleted, and items you already have (same title in the same category) are skipped. Lists with the same name are joined.

Moving from Android

  1. In Hammock for Android, open Settings → Your data → Export backup and save the file to Google Drive, or share it to yourself by email.
  2. On your iPhone, save the file to Files (from Mail, tap the attachment and choose Save to Files; from Google Drive, use Open in, then Save to Files).
  3. In Hammock, open Settings → Your data → Import backup and choose the file.

Moving the other way works the same, because both apps read and write the same format. Hammock Plus is bought separately on each platform's store.

Themes and categories

Settings → Appearance has eight colour themes: Linen and Honey are free; Terracotta, Meadow, Ocean, Plum, Rose and Ink come with Plus. Each has a light version and a dusk version that is never pure black. Choose System, Light or Dark to follow your iPhone or fix one. The share sheet and the widgets follow your theme too.

Settings → Categories lets you rename, recolour and re-icon any category, change what its progress is counted in, and hide the ones you do not use (their items stay in your library). With Plus you can add your own, such as Recipes or Courses; deleting one moves its items to Other.

Privacy

Hammock has no account and keeps your library on your device. It uses the internet only when you search a catalogue (your search words go to that catalogue), save or open a link, or when covers are downloaded from where they are published. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". The full details are in the privacy policy.

Accessibility

  • VoiceOver reads every cover as its title, creator, category, progress and rating. On the rating stars, swipe up or down to change the rating by half a star; on the progress card, swipe up or down to step progress. In the Pile, the rotor's Actions offer Start, Mark done and Delete.
  • Dynamic Type: text grows with your text size setting, and the status picker turns into wrapping buttons when the labels no longer fit side by side.
  • Reduce Motion: Pick for me lands directly without the shuffle.
  • Every action has a haptic you can feel, and every change is also spoken by VoiceOver.

Troubleshooting

Search says "Search did not work"

A catalogue could not be reached. Check your connection and try again; the other catalogues still answer. Apple's catalogue allows a limited number of searches a minute, so a short pause can help.

A cover is missing

Not every catalogue entry has artwork, and some pages do not publish an image. The item still works, with a coloured card and its first letter. Covers you have seen are kept on the device, so they also show offline.

Something I shared is not in the Pile

Shared items arrive when Hammock next opens. If you shared while Hammock was already open, switch away and back. If the sheet closed before you tapped Save, nothing was saved.

The widget says it is part of Hammock Plus

Open Hammock once after buying or restoring Plus; the widget updates within a few seconds.

Plus is not available or the price does not show

The App Store did not answer. Check the connection and that In-App Purchases are allowed in Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions, then open Plus again.

I want to start over

Export a backup first if you might want it later, then delete the app and install it again. Restore Plus from Settings afterwards.

Still stuck? Email support@top7systems.net with your device, iOS version and what you expected to happen. A screenshot helps.
