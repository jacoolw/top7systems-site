Questions

Before you download

Why calculate weight as well as volume?

Many landscape suppliers sell by weight rather than by volume. GravelCalc converts between the two using each material's density, so you can compare quotes and order the right amount however your supplier prices it.

How deep should I spread it?

As a rule of thumb: 2–4 inches for gravel driveways and paths, 2–3 inches for mulch, and 4–6 inches for topsoil in garden beds. The app pre-selects a recommended depth when you choose a material, and the Reference tab carries the full table along with coverage per cubic yard.

What waste factor should I use?

7% is the default and the standard recommendation. Use 5% for simple flat areas, 10% for slopes or irregular shapes, and 15% where compaction is expected. Gravel and decomposed granite compact significantly, so add another 10–15% if you plan to run a plate compactor over them.

Can I calculate several materials in one project?

Yes. Each calculation added to the Project tab remembers its material, so a driveway, a set of beds and a lawn can sit in one project with separate and combined totals.

Does it work offline?

Entirely. There is no account to create and no internet connection required, so it works in the yard, at the supplier, or anywhere you have your phone.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your project, pricing and preferences are saved on your device and are never sent to any server. See the privacy policy. The formulas behind every number are published in full on the support page.
