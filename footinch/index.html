Questions

Straight answers.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Everything runs on your device, including voice recognition. The only network call is Apple's App Store when you buy or restore FootInch Pro.

How do I type 5' 6-3/8"?

Press 5, ft, 6, in, 3, /, 8. A number after ft is taken as inches and a fraction after in is part of that inch. Solver fields accept typed forms too: 5' 6 3/8", 66.375, 6-3/8 and 1676 mm all work.

What does the ruler under the result show?

A two-inch window of tape measure centred on your answer with 1/16" ticks, cumulative inch numbers and red foot marks, exactly like the tape in your pouch. In metric it shows millimetres and centimetres.

Is the math checked?

The engine and every solver are covered by automated tests against worked examples: a 6:12 rafter over a 24' span, a 9' 1" stair rise, a 38° crown at a 90° corner. The formulas are published on the support page so you can verify them.

Will there ever be a subscription?

No. FootInch Pro is a one-time purchase and will stay that way.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, with a full-size keypad and hardware keyboard shortcuts.
