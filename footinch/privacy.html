FootInch — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Introduction

Top 7 Systems ("we", "our", or "us") operates the FootInch mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app.

Data Collection — We Keep It Simple

FootInch is a standalone calculator that runs entirely on your device:

  • No account required. Every feature works without signing up or providing personal information.
  • No personal data collected. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or location.
  • No analytics or tracking. The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs or advertising frameworks.
  • No network requests other than Apple's App Store (StoreKit) when you buy or restore FootInch Pro. Your calculations and your voice never leave your device.

Data Storage

Your calculation tape and preferences are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard on-device storage, and are included in your device backups if you have backups enabled. We never upload, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers.

Microphone and speech recognition

Voice entry is optional. When you tap the mic, FootInch listens until you stop speaking and converts the phrase into a measurement. Recognition runs on your device using Apple's on-device speech engine; audio is never recorded, stored or uploaded, and voice entry is disabled on devices that can't process speech locally. You can revoke the Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Purchases

FootInch Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13. It contains no user accounts, social features, or data collection mechanisms.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
